Competition

Competitive Events

Visit DECA’s Competitive Events page for a complete list of events, sample events, and official guidelines.

Please also note these MSJ DECA restrictions:

  1. Only first-year members may compete in Principles role-play events.
  2. Only returning members may compete in Project Management events after receiving approval from the officer team and Mr. Liu.

Aside from online events such as VBC, SMG, ELC, and other special or online challenges, students may compete in a maximum of two competitive events: one prepared or written event and one series role-play event.

DECA events are organized into five career clusters:

  • Business Management & Administration: Administrative services, business information management, general management, human resources, operations, and business ethics.
  • Marketing: Exploring, creating, and delivering value to meet the needs of a target market. These events often offer creative freedom and opportunities to design marketing campaigns.
  • Finance: Accounting, banking services, business finance, insurance, securities, and investments. Personal Financial Literacy focuses on skills such as budgeting, tracking spending, paying off debt, and planning for retirement.
  • Entrepreneurship: Creating and learning how to run businesses, including start-ups, franchises, international businesses, and business growth plans.
  • Hospitality & Tourism: Marketing and management functions related to hotels, restaurants, tourism, and travel.

Different events use different formats, guidelines, rubrics, and skill sets. Start by thinking about whether you prefer working alone or with a team, presenting with limited preparation, developing a prepared presentation, conducting research, or competing online.

Role-plays

  • For first-year members, Principles events are a popular option.
  • If you like working alone, consider Individual Series or Professional Selling.
  • If you like brainstorming and presenting with a partner, consider Team Decision Making. These events generally provide more preparation time than individual role-plays.

Prepared events

  • If you like creating campaigns for existing products, events, or services, consider an Integrated Marketing Campaign.
  • If you like researching existing companies, developing strategic plans, and writing detailed reports, consider Operations Research.

Online events

  • If you are interested in simulating a business, consider the Virtual Business Challenge.
  • If you are interested in investing, consider the Stock Market Game.

Explore more options at deca.org/compete, or try the DECA Find Your Event quiz.

02

Membership pathway

CTE Members

To be a CTE member, a student must be enrolled in or have completed the Entrepreneurship CTE pathway, which includes both Entrepreneurship and Entrepreneurial Marketing.

Beginning with the 2026–27 school year, students may not take a gap year between the two courses and must complete the pathway in consecutive years.

There is no prerequisite for the first course in the pathway. The second course may only be taken after completing the first.

When classes fill, priority is based on seniority: seniors receive priority first, followed by juniors, sophomores, and freshmen. These classes often have long registration waitlists, so do not be discouraged if you cannot enroll immediately—DECA Spark offers another way to participate.

04

What to expect

Conferences

Competitor schedules and presentation times are generally released shortly before presentations. Arrive at the competition area before your assigned time, locate the table for your event, check in with the volunteer, and wait for your name to be called.

For prepared events, you will be brought to your judge and deliver your prepared presentation. For role-play events, you will receive your prompt and preparation time before presenting to a judge. Judges may ask questions at the end of either type of presentation.

Every event has its own rubric. Prepared and written events are evaluated using their required sections, while role-plays are scored using performance indicators. Find the current rubric for your event on the DECA Competitive Events page.

Your overall event result may combine presentation or written-component scores with a cluster exam score. Because scoring differs by event, always check the current official guidelines for your specific event.

Wear business formal clothing for judged events, including prepared presentations and role-plays. Business casual clothing may be permitted for testing sessions. Follow all conference-specific dress instructions shared by the chapter.
